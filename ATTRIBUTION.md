# Wallpaper sources

All wallpapers in `backgrounds/` were generated for this theme. They are fan-made, Factorio-inspired scenes and are not official Factorio artwork.

The unlock-screen gear uses the Factorio Wiki's 64×64 [iron gear wheel icon](https://wiki.factorio.com/images/Iron_gear_wheel.png), tinted to match the lettering.
